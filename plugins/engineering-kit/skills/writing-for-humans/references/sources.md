# Sources and verification

Research checked on 2026-09-10. The software, specification structure, and issue-tracker linking safeguards are practical engineering adaptations alongside selected ASD-STE100 principles, not additional ASD-STE100 rules.

## Official sources

| Source | What it supports |
| --- | --- |
| [STEMG home page](https://www.asd-ste100.org/) | Issue 9 is dated January 15, 2025. STEMG maintains the standard. |
| [About STE](https://www.asd-ste100.org/about_STE.html) | The standard combines 53 writing rules in nine sections with a controlled dictionary. Approved general words have restricted meanings and parts of speech. Technical nouns and verbs have their own conditions of use. |
| [Issue 9 standard](https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf) | Rule 3.6 covers active voice and the unknown-actor exception in descriptive text. Section 5 covers procedural sentences, commands, conditions, and notes. Rule 6.3 sets the descriptive sentence limit. |
| [Current FAQ](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_faq.html) | STE principles can help general technical writing. It explains terminology, spelling, and limits of simplified writing. |
| [Training guidance](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_training.html) | The official standard remains the reference for correct STE use. Writers need both subject knowledge and language knowledge. |
| [Official downloads](https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html) | Provides the current official-copy request route and STEMG guidance on AI. Clear-sounding AI output does not prove compliance; informed review remains necessary. |

The older `/about.html`, `/request.html`, and `/faq.html` pages still contain Issue 8 information.
Use the current pages linked above when checking the edition.

The initial research used the public STEMG pages and indexed excerpts of the Issue 9 PDF.
Direct PDF retrieval failed during that review. The complete rules and dictionary were not audited or copied into this skill.

## Review beyond the local profile

For a compliance task, obtain the requested issue from the official source and inspect the relevant text directly.
If the PDF link is unavailable, the downloads page provides a request route. Routine writing does not depend on submitting that form.
Recheck the current issue when the user asks for the latest standard; do not assume this dated research proves currentness indefinitely.

Check general words against the dictionary for meaning and part of speech. Check technical nouns and verbs against the standard's categories.
Project approval of a term does not by itself establish that the term qualifies under STE.
Apply the standard's sentence-counting method and grammatical exceptions during a compliance review.

The local profile deliberately allows exact normative wording, source text, and protected identifiers to take precedence over its drafting targets.
Identify such exceptions when assessing compliance. Do not describe the local profile as the complete standard or as an ASD-endorsed checker.
